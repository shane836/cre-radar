Events
The Apartment Association of Greater Los Angeles (AAGLA) offers expert-led education and training for landlords and property owners across Southern California. Members can access weekly legal webinars, networking events, Lunch & Learns, and certificate programs covering rental laws, insurance, compliance, and more.
“AAGLA is a terrific resource for property owners. Their educational programs keep me informed about upcoming legislation and who to contact about potential challenges. The property management webinars and other resources provide invaluable support. I highly recommend AAGLA to any landlord looking to stay ahead in the industry!”
— Janice R., AAGLA Member Since 2023
